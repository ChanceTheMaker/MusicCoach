GPL audio worker from Melodee dc7 via Sloop SMK; see firmware LICENSING.md.
This version uses Felucca source and is not a clean-room implementation.

Build status: FM-1 0.2 USB install/restart was verified; the owner confirmed
screen and key sound. Other controls need hardware testing. Dual-core and
SMK builds remain test candidates. Website publication does not promote them.
Matching GPL source archives and build-specific licensing are linked by the installer.

Candidate 0.2: 87 studies (47 piano, 36 drums, four sequencer workshops),
searchable browser compendium, two generated 16-sample drum banks, optional
animated kit, Coach/Jam/Psychedelic modes, startup mascot/jingle/light show,
12 themes and three appearance modes. Full 27/37-key views with finger colors.
K1 volume, K2 sound, K3 lesson, K4 shuttle; four performance controls over seven
pages. Preferences and stars last for this native session only.

Drum samples are rendered from Felucca's GPL synthesis generator, with original
MusicCoach bank orchestration. Song excerpts identify their public-domain
melodies; other study patterns are MusicCoach originals. The browser app, simulator and installer now share one HTTPS server.
Native hardware does not yet synchronize browser accounts/imports. See docs/MUSICCOACH.md in source.
