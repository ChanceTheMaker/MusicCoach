# Repertoire and curriculum sources

The curated library contains 54 playable teaching arrangements per melodic instrument: 16 primers, 16 lessons (including eight chord/arpeggio studies), 8 original études, 8 familiar melody reductions and 6 original jazz studies. Guitar adds twelve original open-chord and chord-change exercises with newly drawn SVG chord diagrams, giving it 66 playable entries. Drum training adds 34 original rhythm studies, including two coordination counterparts to the harmony curriculum. Thirty jazz-standard title references are indexed for each of the seven instruments, without protected melodies, lyrics or charts. There are 370 playable arrangements and 210 reference entries; these counts do not represent distinct compositions.

Original material is newly written for this project under GPL-3.0-only. The short folk/classical melody reductions are new instructional versions of historical public-domain melodies. Rhythm, octave placement and phrase extent may be simplified; these are not definitive editions, orchestral scores or transcriptions of contemporary recordings. In Frère Jacques, the final bell fifth is raised into the beginner octave. Ode to Joy is the familiar opening theme, not the complete symphony movement.

Historical references:
- Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star: [Library of Congress 1879 score](https://www.loc.gov/resource/music.mussm-sm1879-02893/).
- Frère Jacques and Au clair de la lune: [Library of Congress 1916 recording catalogue](https://www.loc.gov/item/jukebox-18041/). The recording itself is not bundled.
- Ode to Joy: [Mutopia public-domain edition](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=528), Beethoven, maintained by Peter Chubb. Only a new single-line theme reduction is used.
- Mary Had a Little Lamb, Hot Cross Buns, London Bridge and Row, Row, Row Your Boat: traditional nineteenth-century or earlier melodies; individual catalogue entries link to historical text collections for context. No modern arrangements or lyrics are included.
- [Hal Leonard Real Book](https://www.halleonard.com/product/240221/the-real-book-volume-i-sixth-edition): licensed-edition reference for the jazz-standard index. Titles span standard repertoire; membership varies by volume and edition. Index entries never imply a bundled chart or a licence to reproduce it.

User imports are separate origin=user records and are not overwritten by seeding. They stay in the local PostgreSQL volume and are not submitted to a shared/public catalogue.

This is a broad beginner starter curriculum, not a complete graded method, complete Real Book collection or a substitute for instrument-specific teacher review. Timing, durations, ranges and parser structure have automated validation; phrasing, technique and musical editions need human review before any formal teaching claim.
