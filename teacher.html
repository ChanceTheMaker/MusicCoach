WELCOME TO YOUR PRACTICE ROOM

Small steps. Real progress.

  1. Choose your instrument and a lesson.
  2. Use USB MIDI for a keyboard, or select a microphone / interface and connect it. Mic access starts only when you click Connect.
  3. Learn: Wait pauses for correct notes; Demo plays the lesson at the selected tempo without scoring. Practice and Perform keep time. Lower the tempo and repeat a lesson to practice a tricky passage.
  4. Computer keys A W S E D F T G Y H U J K play the first octave of the exercise. Release between repeated notes.

Microphone mode is experimental and recognizes one note at a time. Use headphones and a clean signal. Play a brief gap between repeated notes. Chords, bends, tablature and fingering assessment are future work. Input audio stays in this browser and is never recorded or uploaded.
