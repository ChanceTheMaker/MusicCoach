This app extracts a subset of the rendered notes into JSON data, without changing the MP3 bytes. Other pitches are reproduced by adjusting playback rate. The central piano range keeps every semitone; the other ranges use samples three semitones apart. No third-party JavaScript is executed. The source URLs follow:
https://gleitz.github.io/midi-js-soundfonts/FluidR3_GM/INSTRUMENT-mp3.js

Included instruments: acoustic_grand_piano, acoustic_guitar_steel, electric_bass_finger, alto_sax, tenor_sax, soprano_sax, baritone_sax, harmonica.
